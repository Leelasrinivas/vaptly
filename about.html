Our Conviction

Why Vaptly exists

Most security firms sell compliance. Vaptly sells reduction of actual risk.

The distinction matters when a nation-state actor is in your network at 2am and your firewall logs look clean. Compliance is a snapshot. Security is a continuous adversarial contest.

We were built by practitioners who have worked inside defense-critical environments, responded to real breaches under operational pressure, and understand that the gap between "passing an audit" and "actually being secure" is where most organisations live — and where attackers thrive.

Every service, every product, and every engagement at Vaptly is designed to close that gap permanently — not paper over it.

Manual, adversarial, every time

No automated scans repackaged as assessments. Every engagement involves a human operator pursuing real attack paths against your actual environment.

Zero false positives — guaranteed

Every finding is validated with proof-of-concept exploitation before delivery. Your team remediates real vulnerabilities, not theoretical flags.

No junior consultants on your engagement

Every client engagement is led by a senior operator with direct, field-proven experience. You never work through an account manager to reach your security team.

A decade at the edge of adversarial defense

Leela Srinivas Atla brings over a decade of hands-on offensive security experience, having served as a security architect for some of the world's most sensitive defense and aerospace environments. He has led penetration testing and security architecture engagements for the Department of Defense, the United States Navy, NASA, and the Air Force.

His operational background spans red team operations, critical infrastructure protection, zero-trust architecture design, and adversary simulation at both classified and unclassified levels. Leela founded Vaptly to bring that same rigor to enterprise clients who deserve more than checkbox security.

Beyond practice, Leela is a recognised voice in cybersecurity education — a recurring guest speaker at IIT Tirupati, IIT Madras, IIT Kharagpur, and VIT, engaging the next generation of security professionals on adversarial thinking, ethical hacking, and building careers in offensive security.

How We Work

Three principles we never compromise

These are not corporate values on a wall. They are operational rules that determine what every Vaptly engagement looks like from first contact to final delivery.

01

Adversarial First

We think like attackers because we have been attackers. Every assessment begins with the question: how would we breach this? Then we answer it with proof, not conjecture.

02

No False Assurance

We do not produce reports designed to make you feel secure. We produce evidence of your actual risk exposure with proof-of-concept exploits, and we close every gap we open.

03

Operator Mindset

Speed, precision, and minimal noise. We embed with your team rather than delivering a report and disappearing. Your security posture improves continuously — not in quarterly snapshots.
