Before You Submit

Frequently asked questions

Is the initial assessment really at no cost?
Yes. The scoping call and preliminary risk assessment are complimentary and carry no obligation to proceed. We conduct this so both parties can make an informed decision about fit before any commitment is made.
Will I be speaking directly with a senior operator?
Always. Vaptly does not route enquiries through sales development representatives or junior consultants. Your first call will be with the operator or architect who would lead your engagement.
How quickly can an engagement begin after scoping?
For most assessment types, we can begin within five to ten business days of signing the SOW — subject to availability. Emergency incident response retainers can be activated within one hour of contract execution.
Do you work with organisations outside the United States?
Yes. We operate globally. Remote engagements are available immediately and we deploy on-site operators internationally for red team and incident response engagements with appropriate advance notice.
What information should I include in my submission?
A high-level description of your environment (cloud, on-premises, or hybrid), the service or product you are interested in, any active compliance requirements, and your preferred engagement timeline. You do not need to share sensitive technical details before the NDA is in place.
What happens to the information I submit?
Your submission is reviewed only by the Vaptly operator assigned to your enquiry. We do not share, sell, or distribute contact information to third parties under any circumstances. A mutual NDA is executed before any detailed technical information is exchanged.
