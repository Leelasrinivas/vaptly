Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Straightforward answers to the questions we hear most often from security leaders and procurement teams.

How long does a typical VAPT engagement take?
Scope determines duration. A focused web application assessment typically completes in 5–10 business days. A full-infrastructure red team engagement ranges from three to six weeks. We provide a precise timeline during the scoping call before any work begins.
Do you perform testing in production environments?
Yes, with explicit written authorisation and defined rules of engagement. We conduct all production testing during agreed maintenance windows and implement safeguards to prevent service disruption throughout the engagement.
What does the GRC engagement process look like?
We begin with a current-state assessment against your target framework, produce a prioritised remediation roadmap, then work alongside your team to implement controls and collect evidence. Engagements typically run 8–16 weeks to certification-ready status.
How quickly can you respond to an active breach?
Retainer clients receive operator response within 15 minutes. For non-retainer engagements, our average initial response time is under two hours. We strongly recommend establishing a retainer before an incident occurs — response speed is the single largest factor in containment cost.
Is the security awareness training self-service or managed?
Fully managed. We configure the platform, build campaign content tailored to your industry, schedule simulations, and deliver monthly reporting. Your team receives a dashboard and quarterly executive briefing — no internal administration required.
Can services be combined into a single engagement?
Absolutely. Many of our clients begin with VAPT to identify technical exposure, layer GRC advisory to address framework gaps, and add training to reduce the human risk surface — all coordinated under a single account team and master service agreement.
