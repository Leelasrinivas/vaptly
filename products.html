PhishNet IQ deploys realistic, context-aware phishing campaigns across your organisation — in 50+ languages, across every department. The platform scores each employee's risk profile in real time and routes them to targeted micro-training based on their actual behaviour.

This is not a click-tracking exercise. PhishNet IQ builds a living map of your organisation's human attack surface, updated continuously as threat behaviours evolve.

Campaign Engine

Risk Scoring

Adaptive Training

02 Network Detection & Response

Sentinelyx

Predictive network detection and response

Sentinelyx continuously analyses network traffic, endpoint telemetry, and log streams to identify breach activity before data is exfiltrated. The AI engine correlates weak signals across multiple vectors — turning noise into actionable, MITRE-mapped threat intelligence.

Unlike traditional SIEMs that require months of tuning, Sentinelyx achieves meaningful detection coverage from day one using pre-trained behavioural models specific to your industry.

Predictive Correlation

Connects subtle multi-stage signals that individually appear benign but collectively indicate breach activity.

MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

Lateral Movement Detection

Identifies attackers traversing your network in real time before they reach critical assets.

1-Click Incident Response

Trigger a ticket, isolate an endpoint, and notify your response team from a single action panel.

03 Credential Security

VaultEx

Zero-knowledge encrypted credential vault

VaultEx is built around the principle that even your security vendor should never have access to your credentials. A zero-knowledge architecture means all encryption and decryption happens on-device — VaultEx servers store only ciphertext that you control.

Deployed in under 48 hours with full enterprise SSO, SCIM provisioning, and continuous dark web monitoring. FIPS 140-2 validated cryptographic modules throughout.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Your master key never leaves your device. Vaptly cannot access or recover stored credentials.

Enterprise SSO & SCIM

Native SAML 2.0 and OIDC with Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, and all major identity providers.

FIPS 140-2 Compliance

All cryptographic operations use validated modules — required for federal and healthcare deployments.

Dark Web Alerts

Continuous monitoring of breach databases and dark web forums with instant employee notifications.
