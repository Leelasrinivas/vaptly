01 People Risk Platform

PhishNet IQ

Security awareness and phishing simulation for organisations that want measurable improvement, not box-checking.

PhishNet IQ helps you understand which teams, users, and workflows are most exposed to phishing and social engineering. It runs realistic exercises, measures responses, and shows you where extra guidance is needed.

Instead of long annual training that people ignore, the platform supports short, targeted learning based on actual user behaviour. That makes the programme easier to manage and easier for employees to complete.

Realistic simulations

Run phishing, SMS, and voice-based exercises that reflect real attacks employees might face.

Clear risk view

See which users or departments need more support through easy-to-read scoring and dashboards.

Short targeted learning

Assign short lessons after failed tests so employees improve without losing hours of productivity.

Exposure monitoring

Receive alerts if employee credentials appear in known breach sources or exposed data sets.

02 Threat Detection Platform

Sentinelyx

A network detection and response platform designed to help security teams see important threats faster and respond with less noise.

Sentinelyx brings network activity, system signals, and suspicious behaviour into one view so teams can focus on what matters. It helps analysts identify patterns that might otherwise be missed when information is spread across too many tools.

The goal is simple: fewer wasted hours, clearer alerts, and faster action when something looks wrong. That makes it easier for lean security teams to stay effective.

Better signal quality

Bring together multiple weak indicators so your team can focus on real suspicious activity.

Prioritised alerts

Give analysts helpful context so they can understand severity quickly and respond with confidence.

Movement tracking

Spot suspicious internal movement before attackers reach sensitive systems or data.

Fast response support

Start key response actions quickly from the same interface instead of jumping across systems.

03 Credential Security Platform

VaultEx

A secure credential platform that helps organisations control access, protect sensitive logins, and reduce password-related risk.

VaultEx gives teams a safer way to store and share business credentials without relying on spreadsheets, chat messages, or personal password habits. It helps centralise control while keeping access practical for employees and administrators.

It is especially useful for growing companies that need better password hygiene, clearer ownership, and stronger oversight across teams, vendors, and privileged accounts.

Secure storage

Store company credentials in one protected place instead of passing them around informally.

Access control

Assign access by role, team, or responsibility so only the right people can reach sensitive credentials.

Stronger policy enforcement

Set rules for password quality, sharing, and account protection across the organisation.

Exposure alerts

Receive visibility when business credentials may have been exposed and need immediate attention.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers for teams evaluating which product to adopt first.

Which product should we start with?
Start with the problem causing the biggest risk today. If phishing and employee mistakes are the concern, begin with PhishNet IQ. If you need better threat visibility, start with Sentinelyx. If password sharing and access control are weak, start with VaultEx.
Can we buy one product first and add others later?
Yes. Each product works on its own, and you can expand into the full suite later as your programme grows.
Are these products only for large enterprises?
No. They are designed for enterprise needs, but they are also suitable for growing companies that need clearer controls and better visibility without adding complexity.
How long does onboarding usually take?
Most teams can begin onboarding quickly after scoping and approval. The exact timeline depends on environment size, integrations, and internal review requirements.
Do you provide demos before purchase?
Yes. We provide live demos so your stakeholders can understand how the product works, what it solves, and what implementation would look like.
Can these products support compliance efforts?
Yes. While each product solves an operational security problem first, they also support stronger controls, visibility, and evidence that can help broader compliance efforts.
